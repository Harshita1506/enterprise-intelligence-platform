1. Sprint Executive Summary
Sprint 14 was a challenging but productive iteration. The team successfully finalized the frontend UI for the loyalty dashboard and completely wrapped up the Auth0 SSO integration, pulling us slightly ahead of schedule on the frontend deliverables. However, backend progress was bottlenecked. The Stripe backend integration is currently stalled at 65% completion due to persistent rate limit bugs in our CI/CD pipeline, and the SAP data synchronization tasks were delayed by infrastructure downtime. Overall sprint completion stands at 78% of committed story points.

5. Current Blockers & Issues

Stripe API Rate Limits: Our automated testing suite generates synthetic transactions faster than the Stripe sandbox allows on the standard tier. This is causing CI/CD pipeline failures, preventing backend merges to the staging branch.

SAP Staging Instability: The legacy SAP staging environment experienced a 48-hour unannounced outage. Developers were unable to query test data, severely slowing down ticket NOVA-433.

6. Technical Debt & Quality Notes

Due to the SAP rate limit constraints identified this sprint, we have to introduce a Redis caching layer (NOVA-450) into Sprint 15. This wasn't originally sized in the architecture plan and represents a slight accumulation of architectural debt that we must immediately address to prevent production outages.

Two High-priority bugs were logged regarding React hydration mismatches on the order history page. Alex Kumar has allocated 4 hours in Sprint 15 to resolve these.
