1. Executive Summary & Current Health

Overall Project Status: YELLOW (Caution - Technical Blockers)

Budget Health: GREEN (On Track)

Timeline Health: YELLOW (At Risk of 2-week slip for Go-Live)

Scope: GREEN (Phase 1 scope maintained)

Project CareFlow is advancing rapidly on the patient-facing deliverables, but we have encountered severe integration friction with our legacy systems. The frontend React web and mobile applications are functionally complete and have passed crucial accessibility audits. However, the project timeline is currently marked as Yellow due to critical scalability failures within the on-premise CareSync EHR system when handling automated booking requests. We are engineering a robust workaround, but this requires executive intervention with the vendor to ensure long-term stability.

2. Major Achievements

Patient Experience Complete: The user interface for searching providers, selecting appointments, and dynamic waitlisting has been finalized. Feedback from early clinical UX testing has been overwhelmingly positive.

Omnichannel Reminders: Integration with Twilio for automated SMS and voice calls is successfully built, allowing us to hit patients with reminders at 72, 24, and 2-hour intervals.

Accessibility Milestone: The patient portal officially passed WCAG 2.1 AA compliance testing, ensuring equitable access for all patients.

3. Key Blockers & Risks

CareSync EHR Limitations (Critical Blocker): Load testing this week revealed that the CareSync database cannot handle concurrent scheduling requests. It locks and fails when multiple patients attempt to book simultaneously. This prevents us from executing a real-time booking integration.

Architectural Pivot (Technical Debt): To bypass the CareSync limitation, the engineering team is building an asynchronous message queue (RabbitMQ) to slowly feed bookings into the legacy system. This adds complexity and introduces a risk of race conditions (double-bookings) that we must carefully manage through the user interface.

Telecommunications Compliance: Approval for our dedicated SMS short code is stalled with major cellular carriers. We are pivoting to standard 10-digit numbers for Go-Live to avoid delays.

4. Executive Recommendations & Escalations

Vendor Escalation (Action Required): I urgently request that Rohan Sharma and the IT procurement team formally escalate the database locking issue to CareSync’s executive leadership. While our RabbitMQ queue acts as a band-aid, CareSync must patch their FHIR APIs to support modern concurrency, or the system will continually bottleneck clinical operations.

HIPAA & Privacy Endorsement: The introduction of a message broker requires application-layer encryption for ePHI in transit. We require expedited review and sign-off from the corporate Data Privacy Officer on our revised security architecture by September 18th to avoid blocking development.

5. Upcoming Milestones (Next 14 Days)

September 22: Complete refactoring of the booking API utilizing the new asynchronous queue architecture.

September 25: Conduct end-to-end load testing simulating 5,000 concurrent morning-rush users to validate the RabbitMQ integration.

September 30: Milestone 3 Deadline - Twilio SMS & Waitlist Engine (On track, pending 10DLC registration).

6. Client/Business Expectations
Clinic directors should be advised that due to the legacy EHR limitations, patients will experience a "Booking Pending" state when utilizing the new portal. The system will securely queue their request and text them a final confirmation once the legacy system accepts the appointment. While a slight deviation from the original "instant book" vision, this approach guarantees system stability and prevents total EHR outages during peak hours.
