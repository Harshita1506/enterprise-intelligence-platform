1. Sprint Executive Summary
Sprint 12 yielded highly polarized results. The engineering team achieved massive success integrating the Checkr API and the DocuSign eSignature scaffolding, proving out our event-driven Kafka architecture. However, the AI resume parsing engine completely failed its internal legal and compliance review due to identified algorithmic biases regarding document formatting and inferred demographics. This required a major mid-sprint scope change to remove all automated rejection capabilities. Consequently, significant engineering effort is now being redirected toward re-tuning the AI pre-processing layer, causing several backend tickets to spill over.

5. Current Blockers & Issues

AI Compliance (Critical Blocker): The discovery that our PDF text extraction scrambles multi-column resumes, thereby artificially lowering the AI fit score for certain candidates, is a major issue. We are pivoting to an OCR-first approach, which is significantly more computationally expensive and requires architecture changes.

Workday Throttling: Workday is rejecting our batch synchronization attempts. We must write a custom paginated polling mechanism with backoff logic to slowly sip data from their HRIS without triggering their WAF (Web Application Firewall).

6. Technical Debt & Quality Notes

Mocked Workday Data: To unblock the frontend team, we have hardcoded mock Workday organizational data into the staging environment. This technical debt must be resolved in Sprint 13, or we risk a catastrophic failure during end-to-end integration testing.

Removing the auto-reject feature required surgically ripping out several Kafka event streams. The team needs to allocate time in Sprint 13 to clean up dead code and unused database columns.
